## Platform

## Stack

React + Vite (existing codebase)

## Users

Primary: Homeowners in Burnsville, Minnesota experiencing HVAC issues (emergency breakdowns, routine maintenance needs, system replacements). Secondary: Property managers, landlords.

Situation: HVAC failure is urgent, stressful, and often unexpected. Users need fast, trustworthy service from a local company that feels personal—not a corporate call center.

Job: Get heating/cooling restored quickly, at fair pricing, by technicians who treat their home and family with respect.

## Product Purpose

Northline Heating & Air provides reliable residential HVAC services (repair, installation, maintenance, 24/7 emergency) to Burnsville, MN homeowners. Success means: same-day service when promised, transparent pricing, technicians who show up on time and communicate clearly, and customers who call back and refer neighbors.

## Positioning

"Your family's comfort comes first." — A family-owned neighborhood HVAC company that prioritizes reliability, honesty, and personal service over scale. The mechanism: same-day service guarantee (diagnostic fee free if not met) backed by 24/7 emergency availability—claims a corporate competitor cannot truthfully copy because they lack the local ownership and dispatch flexibility.

## Operating Context

- Service area: Burnsville, Minnesota only
- Technicians dispatched from local base
- Phone-first contact (no online booking, no chat)
- Seasonal demand spikes: heating emergencies in winter, AC failures in summer
- Trust signals matter: family-owned, 24/7 availability, same-day guarantee
- No financing offers, no free estimates, no invented certifications

## Product Principles

1. **Trust over flash** — Every design choice reinforces reliability and honesty; no marketing gimmicks.
2. **Local feel, professional execution** — Warm, family-oriented tone delivered with modern, polished craft.
3. **Phone-first urgency** — The primary action is always a tap-to-call; no friction between problem and contact.
4. **Clarity at a glance** — Services, guarantee, and emergency availability understood within seconds.
5. **Restraint with purpose** — Animations, color, and layout serve the user's task; nothing decorative for its own sake.
